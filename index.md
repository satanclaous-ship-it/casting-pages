# Casting

**Cast it. Feel it. Shift it.**

A spoken-affirmation ritual. 30-second subconscious rewiring.

Casting is a daily voice ritual where you speak affirmations out loud to interrupt the thought loops that limit you and root ones that affirm you — rewiring patterns from the inside out.

Each ritual: **cast** (speak) → **feel** (inner sensation) → **shift** (the pattern moves). Repeated daily, shifts compound into who you become.

The inside changes; the world reflects it back.
